# Ironclad BJJ Academy

> Family-first gi and no-gi BJJ website template built to fill kids programs and grow adult memberships.

## Live Demo

See it in action: [https://creatiquemedia.com/templates/ironclad-bjj/demo](https://creatiquemedia.com/templates/ironclad-bjj/demo)

## Who It Is For

Kids and family BJJ academies, gi and no-gi schools, grappling clubs, and mixed-program martial arts gyms.

A modern academy website with dedicated kids and family program sections, gi curriculum tracks, no-gi sport pages, coach credibility blocks, and trial-funnel conversion paths.

## What's Included

**Key sections and features:**

- Class schedule section: a weekly timetable with kids, gi, no-gi, and open mat blocks
- Family and kids positioning with parent-facing copy, intro nights, and program descriptions
- Events calendar for seminars, intro nights, and academy updates
- Tournament promotion cards with competition dates, team registration notes, and competitor callouts
- Promotion milestone blocks for belt promotions and stripe weeks
- Modern no-gi brand feel and local academy credibility cues

## Design & Tech Highlights
